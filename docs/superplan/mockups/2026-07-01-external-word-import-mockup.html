How the Brain Learns Language

Recent studies show that connection and repetition are the two most important factors in vocabulary retention. When you encounter a word in a meaningful context, your brain forms a richer memory trace.

For example, the word serendipity — finding something good without looking for it — is much easier to remember when attached to a personal story. Try to notice new words while reading articles, then add them to your deck.
